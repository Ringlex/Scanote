## Full description (max 4000)

```
Scanote turns a photograph of a page into a note you can search, edit and check off.

Point the camera at a lecture page, a receipt, a recipe or a handwritten list. The text
is recognised on your phone — no page you photograph is uploaded anywhere for that to
happen, and it works with the phone in flight mode. The photograph stays with the note,
so you can always go back and read what the recognition could not.

A scanned list arrives as a checklist. Tick the items off as you go.

WHAT IT DOES

• Scan a page and get its text — recognition runs on the device, offline
• Keep several pages in one note, photographs and all
• Search reaches inside your photographs, not just what you typed
• A scanned list becomes a checklist you can tick off
• Headings, bold and lists that render in the note, not raw symbols
• Dictate a note when your hands are busy
• Categories and favourites to keep the pile in order
• A calendar with dates and reminders, in the same app
• A home screen widget for what you wrote last
• Share a note as a QR code another phone can read
• Light and dark themes
• Polish and English

NOTES ONLY YOUR FINGERPRINT OPENS

Mark a note as protected and it is encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key held in the
Android Keystore. That key never leaves the phone — not in a backup, not to us, not to
anyone. Opening the note asks for your fingerprint or screen lock.

Because the key stays on the phone, protected notes are deliberately left out of sync.
They live on the phone you wrote them on.

SYNC AND BACKUP, IF YOU WANT THEM

Sync is optional and off until you turn it on. When you do, your notes travel through
your own Google Drive — Scanote asks only for access to the files it created, never to
the rest of your Drive. Scans and protected notes stay on the phone.

You can also export the whole database to a file and import it back on another phone.

A DELETED NOTE IS NOT GONE

Deleted notes wait in the bin for thirty days before they are removed for good.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED

Scanote works without signing in to anything. A Google account is asked for only if you
switch sync on, and only for the Drive files Scanote itself created.

There are no ads.
```
